Waypoints

See who's out. Find the meet. Show the build.

Waypoints is a live map for car people, on iPhone. Your friends show up where they are; everyone else sees nothing unless you say so.

  • Drop a meet on the map in three fields.
  • Report speed bumps, steep entrances, and closures, and get routes scored for a lowered car.
  • Drive in a convoy with a shared route and a regroup point.
  • Talk to your team in text and voice channels.
  • Put your real car in the garage and log the build.
  • Buy and sell parts.

Get Waypoints

Waypoints is on the App Store for iPhone. Search for Waypoints.

Nothing is for sale. Rankings are votes. Nobody ever sees your speed.
