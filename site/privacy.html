Privacy Policy

What Waypoints collects, who sees it, and what is never kept.

Your location

Your location puts you on the map for the people you choose. You set who sees you, per audience, in Settings, Privacy and location:

  • Friends: precise by default. Your real position, live, with heading.
  • Teams: approximate by default. A half-mile area, updated at most every 15 seconds, with no heading.
  • Drivers nearby: hidden by default. Can be approximate, never precise.
  • A convoy you join sees your exact position while the convoy runs. The join screen says so.

Approximate positions are snapped on our server before they leave it, so a person who isn't allowed your precise location never receives it. Hidden sends nothing; you look offline.

Ghost mode hides you from everyone. Home zones cap everyone at approximate and hide you from drivers nearby; a zone's centre is stored only on your phone and is held in memory by our server while you're connected, never written to a database.

Background location is used only if you turn on "Keep sharing when the app is closed". It stops after 5 minutes parked.

No location history

Our server keeps no track of where you've been. It stores one coarse cell (about 5 square kilometres) per person, so it can tell you about meets nearby. Drive traces used for road ratings stay on your phone unless you save a stretch of road.

Your speed

Your speed appears only on your own navigation screen. It is never sent to anyone else.

What else we store

  • Your email, for one-time sign-in codes.
  • Your profile, cars, builds, photos, posts, meets, reports, listings, and messages, so the people you share them with can see them.
  • Your privacy settings, friends, teams, and blocks.
  • Reports you send and reports about you, for moderation.
  • A push notification token, if you allow notifications.

No analytics, no ads, no tracking

Waypoints has no analytics or advertising SDKs and doesn't track you across apps or websites. We don't sell your data. Crash and performance diagnostics come from Apple's MetricKit, stay on your phone, and are shared with us only through Apple if you choose to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings.

Camera, microphone, photos, calendar

The camera reads your VIN and takes photos you choose to post. The microphone is used only in your team's voice channels. Waypoints saves to your photo library and calendar only when you ask it to.

Who we share with

Data is stored with our hosting providers (Supabase for the database and files) only to run the app. Maps and routes use Apple Maps. We share data with authorities only when the law requires it.

Deleting your data

Delete your account in Settings, Account. Your profile, cars, posts, messages you sent, and listings are deleted right away.

Age

Waypoints is for people 17 and older.

Changes and contact

We'll update this page when what we collect changes. Questions: support@waypoints.app
