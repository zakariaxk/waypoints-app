# Waypoints — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: February 27, 2026**

## Overview

Waypoints ("the App") is a real-time location sharing application that allows users to create and join temporary sessions to share their location with friends and groups.

## Information We Collect

### Location Data
- The App collects your device's GPS location **only while the App is actively in use** and you are participating in a session.
- Location data is shared in real time with other participants in your current session.
- **We do not collect location data in the background.**
- **We do not store location history.** Only your most recent location is held in temporary server memory during an active session.

### Display Name
- You provide a display name when creating or joining a session. This is stored locally on your device for convenience.
- Display names are visible to other session participants.

### Session Data
- Session data (join codes, participant lists, chat messages) exists only in server memory during the session lifetime.
- **All session data is automatically deleted** when the session ends or when the server restarts. No data is persisted to any database.

### Device Storage
- The App stores your display name and a history of recent sessions locally on your device using AsyncStorage.
- This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time.

## How We Use Your Information

- **Location sharing**: To display your location to other participants in your session on a shared map.
- **Presence indicators**: To show whether participants are active, idle, or offline.
- **Distance/ETA**: To calculate and display distance and estimated time to a session destination.
- **Session management**: To facilitate creating, joining, and managing group sessions.

## Data Sharing

- Your location and display name are shared **only with participants in sessions you choose to join**.
- We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties.
- We do not use your data for advertising or analytics.

## Data Retention

- **Session data**: Automatically deleted when the session ends (all sessions are temporary).
- **Server-side**: No persistent database. All data is in-memory and lost on server restart.
- **Device-side**: Display name and session history stored locally. Delete the app to remove all local data.

## Security

- Communication between the App and our servers uses WebSocket connections.
- Session tokens are randomly generated and unique per session.
- Only participants with valid tokens can access session data.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Your Rights

- **Access**: You can see all data the App has about you — it's displayed in the App itself.
- **Deletion**: Leave a session to stop sharing. Uninstall the App to delete all local data.
- **Opt-out**: Close the App at any time to stop all location sharing.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

## Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository or contact us at the email provided in the App Store listing.
