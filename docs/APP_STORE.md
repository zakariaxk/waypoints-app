# App Store listing and review notes

## Listing

**Name:** Waypoints
**Subtitle:** Car meets, friends, and builds

**Description**

See who's out. Find the meet. Show the build.

Waypoints is a live map for car people. Your friends show up where they are; everyone else sees nothing unless you say so. Drop a meet on the map in three fields. Report speed bumps, steep entrances, and closures, and get routes scored for a lowered car. Drive in a convoy with a shared route and a regroup point when the group splits. Talk to your team in text and voice channels. Put your real car in the garage, log the build, and see and hear what a mod does in the simulator. Judge builds in Showdown. Buy and sell parts.

Nothing is for sale. Rankings are votes. Nobody ever sees your speed.

**Keywords:** car meet, car club, cars and coffee, convoy, garage, mods, lowered, car spotting

## Review notes

**Location.** Location puts the user on the map for people they choose. Defaults: friends precise, teams approximate, nearby strangers hidden. Approximate positions are snapped on the server to a roughly half-mile area before they leave it, so a viewer who is not entitled to precise location never receives it. Ghost mode hides the user from everyone; home zones (stored only on the device) cap everyone at approximate. Background location is used only when the user turns on "Keep sharing when the app is closed" in Privacy and location; it stops after 5 minutes parked. The server stores only a coarse cell (about 5 km²) per user, for "meets near me" notifications. There is no location history on the server.

**Speed.** The user's own speed appears only on their own navigation screen. It is never sent to other users.

**Racing.** The Strip is a virtual drag strip game. The lobby, the time slip, and shared images all say "virtual". It reads no GPS, real speed, or real location. Nothing on the map refers to racing, and no challenge can reference a place or time. Results feed only cosmetic marker icons.

**User content.** Every user, post, message, listing, meet, and report can be reported; users can be blocked from their profile, from the map, and from any message. Blocking hides the user's content immediately. Reports reach a moderation queue reviewed within 24 hours. Terms with the six rules are accepted before any content is shown. Contact: support@waypoints.app.

**Demo account.** Provide an email address for one-time-code sign in in App Store Connect; the code arrives by email.
