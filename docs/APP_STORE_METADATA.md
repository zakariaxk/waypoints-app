## Description
Waypoints is a real-time location sharing app that makes it easy to stay connected with your group. Create a session, share the code, and instantly see everyone on a live map.

**Key Features:**

• **Live Map** — See all participants on a real-time map with colored markers showing their status
• **Instant Sessions** — Create a session in seconds and share a 6-character join code
• **Presence Tracking** — Know who's active, idle, or offline at a glance
• **Group Destination** — Host sets a destination everyone can see with distance and ETA
• **Session Chat** — Send messages to your group without leaving the map
• **Auto-Reconnect** — Seamless reconnection if you lose signal
• **Privacy First** — No accounts, no history, no tracking. Sessions are temporary and all data is deleted when they end.

Perfect for:
- Road trips and convoys
- Music festivals and events
- Group hiking and outdoor activities
- Meeting up with friends in a new city
- Any time you need to keep track of your group

No sign-up required. No ads. No tracking. Just open the app, create a session, and share the code.
